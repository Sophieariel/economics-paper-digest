# AI & Labor Markets Papers — 2026-07-29

## 1. CollabSkill: Evaluating Human-Agent Collaboration On Real-World Tasks [Skills & Tasks]

**Authors:** Yijia Shao, Zora Zhiruo Wang, Neel Ahuja, Yicheng Wang, Bowen Liu, Diyi Yang
**Date:** Submitted April 20, 2026; revised July 25, 2026
**Source:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.09833
**Summary:** The authors build CollabSkill, a framework that pairs real workers with AI agents on tasks matched to each worker's occupation and uses a Bayesian skill-rating system to separate the human and agent contributions to task performance. Drawing on over 1,500 prompts from 386 working sessions across 93 workers, they find that agent rankings under real human collaboration diverge from rankings on fully autonomous benchmarks, and that hands-on collaboration experience, more than any other factor, drives how well workers work with AI. This matters because it offers a way to measure worker productivity gains from AI assistance directly from real occupational tasks rather than from proxy benchmarks, and it points to on-the-job experience as a lever for building AI literacy.
