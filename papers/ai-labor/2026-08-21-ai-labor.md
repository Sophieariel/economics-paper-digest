## 1. The Value of Prompting Skills [Skills & Tasks]

**Authors:** Lara Fleck, Dominik Becker, Marie-Christine Fregin, Andries de Grip, Harald Pfeifer, Kathrin Weis
**Date:** Posted August 2026
**Source:** https://www.iza.org/publications/dp/18871/the-value-of-prompting-skills
**Summary:** The authors run a discrete-choice hiring experiment with German hiring decision-makers, varying fictitious applicants' AI-prompting competency alongside occupation-specific skills, interpersonal ability, gender, and pay expectations. Prompting competency raises hiring odds by about 4% and commands a roughly 2% wage premium on average, rising to about 9% at large firms and AI-adopting firms, and it can partially compensate for weaker social skills but not for missing domain-specific technical skills. The results suggest AI prompting is emerging as a distinct, priced labor-market skill that upgrades job requirements rather than simply substituting for existing ones.
